If you've been researching ways to tame frizzy or wavy hair, nanoplastia has probably come up. It's one of the newer names in salon smoothing treatments, and people are often unsure how it differs from keratin or smoothening. A nanoplastia treatment is designed to make frizzy, wavy or curly hair smoother and straighter, with a silky, natural-looking finish.

Read more

This guide explains what nanoplastia does, who it suits, how it compares with other treatments, what happens at your appointment and how to look after your hair afterwards.

What Is a Nanoplastia Treatment?

Nanoplastia is a smoothing and straightening treatment for frizzy, wavy or curly hair. The goal is hair that's noticeably straighter and smoother, but still soft and natural-looking rather than stiff or flat.

Read more

People usually choose it because they want:

  • Less frizz, especially in humid weather
  • Straighter hair without a poker-straight, stiff look
  • Silky, manageable hair that styles quickly
  • A natural finish that moves well

Who Is Nanoplastia Right For?

A nanoplastia treatment is a good fit if:

Read more
  • Your hair is frizzy, wavy or curly
  • You want it noticeably straighter, not just less frizzy
  • You want a silky, natural-looking finish
  • Daily blow drying and ironing take too long
  • Humidity makes your hair hard to control

It may not be the right choice if you want to keep your waves or volume. In that case, keratin or cysteine may suit you better.

Nanoplastia vs Keratin

This is the most common comparison. Both reduce frizz, but they do different jobs:

Nanoplastia Keratin
Main goal Smoother, straighter hair Frizz control with natural volume
Finish Silky, straighter, natural-looking Smooth, keeps body and movement
Texture change Noticeably straighter Smoother, less frizz
Best for Frizzy, wavy or curly hair Frizzy hair you want to keep full
Read more

In short: if you want your hair straighter, nanoplastia. If you want to keep your volume and lose the frizz, keratin. Our comparison of keratin vs smoothening vs straightening explains keratin in more detail.

What Happens at Your Nanoplastia Appointment

At Xing Premium Unisex Salon, every nanoplastia or cysteine treatment follows a careful process:

  2. Cleanse. Your hair is washed to prepare it.
  3. Treatment applied section by section so every part of your hair is evenly covered.

Tell your stylist about any recent colouring, highlights, previous smoothing treatments, scalp sensitivity or pregnancy. Your history affects which treatment is suitable and how it's planned.

Nanoplastia Aftercare

Good aftercare helps your results last:

Read more
  • Follow your stylist's advice on when to wash your hair for the first time.
  • Use a sulphate-free shampoo and a nourishing conditioner.
  • Use a heat protectant whenever you blow dry, iron or curl.
  • Avoid chlorinated water soon after your treatment.
  • Book a hair spa every few weeks to keep lengths nourished.

Most of the habits in our keratin aftercare guide apply to nanoplastia too, including keeping hair loose in the first few days and washing with lukewarm water.

How Long Does Nanoplastia Last?

It depends on your hair type, how fast it grows and your aftercare. Gentle washing, heat protection and regular hair spas help results last longer. Your stylist will tell you what to expect for your hair and when a follow-up might be needed.

Nanoplastia and Hair Colour

If you colour your hair, plan both services together. The order and spacing of colour and smoothing treatments matter. Tell your stylist about your colour plans at your consultation, and they'll schedule both so your hair stays healthy. Our guide on colouring hair after keratin or smoothening covers the same principles.

Preparing for Your Nanoplastia Appointment

A little preparation helps your appointment go smoothly:

Read more
  • Bring photos of the finish you want, and of looks you don't want.
  • Note your hair history: colour, highlights, henna, previous smoothing treatments and when you had them.
  • Plan around events. Book well before any big occasion so you know how your hair settles.
  • Clear your schedule afterwards so you can follow aftercare in the first few days.
  • Arrive with hair as it normally is, without heavy styling products.

Signs Nanoplastia Might Suit You

If several of these sound familiar, nanoplastia is worth discussing with your stylist.

Read more
  • You spend a long time blow drying or ironing every day
  • Your hair frizzes as soon as you step outside in the monsoon
  • You like straighter hair but dislike a stiff, flat look
  • Your hair is wavy or curly and hard to manage

Common Questions About Nanoplastia

Will nanoplastia make my hair flat? It's designed to give a straighter, silky finish that still looks natural. If keeping volume is your priority, ask about keratin instead.

Read more

Is it suitable for very curly hair? Nanoplastia is designed for frizzy, wavy or curly hair. Your stylist will check your hair and tell you what result to expect.

Can I style my hair afterwards? Yes. Most people find styling much quicker. Always use a heat protectant.

Is it suitable for damaged hair? Your stylist will assess your hair first. If it's very damaged, they may suggest a repairing treatment or hair spa first.

Nanoplastia in the Monsoon

The monsoon is when many people decide to book a smoothing treatment, because humidity makes frizz hard to control. If you're booking during the rains, keep your hair dry for the first few days after your treatment as your stylist advises, carry an umbrella, and use a light serum on damp ends.

Is Nanoplastia Right for You?

Ask yourself:

Read more
  1. Do I want my hair straighter, not just less frizzy? If yes, nanoplastia is worth considering.
  2. Do I want to keep my volume and waves? If yes, keratin or cysteine may suit you better.
  3. Is my hair mainly damaged rather than frizzy? If yes, a repairing hair spa may be the better first step.

If you're unsure, that's what the consultation is for. Your stylist can look at your hair and recommend the right option.
