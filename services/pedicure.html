Quick answers

How often should I get a pedicure?
Every three to four weeks works well for most people and keeps heels smooth and cuticles neat.
What is a heel peel treatment?
A heel peel is a targeted foot treatment that helps soften and smooth rough, dry or cracked-looking heels.
Which pedicure is best for tired feet?
For tired feet, choose the Alga or Tango ritual for a longer, more relaxing massage, or Detox for a deep-cleansing refresh.
What does it cost?
Your stylist confirms the price at consultation, before starting.

Aftercare

Keep Hands & Feet Looking Great

Read moreShow less

  • Moisturise hands and feet daily, especially heels.

  • Wear gloves for cleaning and washing dishes.

  • Use a cuticle oil a few times a week.

  • Let polish dry fully before slipping on closed shoes.

  • Book a manicure or pedicure every 3–4 weeks to keep nails and cuticles neat.

  • Have diabetes, circulation issues, cuts or a nail or skin infection? Tell your therapist before your session.
