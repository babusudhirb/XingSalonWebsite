FAQs

Skin Care Questions

What skin and body services does Xing offer?

Xing offers hydra facials, facials and clean-ups, body polish, waxing, threading and de-tan in Bangalore. These are available at our Sarjapur Road, HSR Layout and Brookfield salons, and your therapist checks your skin first and confirms the treatment suits it.

Which facial should I choose?

The right facial at Xing depends on your skin and what you want from it. A clean-up is a quicker treatment focused on cleansing, while a facial adds massage and nourishment. Your therapist looks at your skin and recommends one before starting.

Is the price confirmed before I start?

Yes. Xing confirms the price of every skin and body service before we start, after your therapist has looked at what you need. Nothing is charged online for a booking, and you pay at the salon after your treatment.

Can I walk in for waxing or threading?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome at all three Xing salons in Bangalore for waxing and threading. Quick services are usually easy to fit in, but message your salon on WhatsApp first if you want a particular time during a busy period.
